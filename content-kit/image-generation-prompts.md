## Image Specifications

**File Formats**:
- **Primary**: WebP for best compression/quality ratio
- **Fallback**: JPG (optimized, 85% quality) for compatibility
- **Transparency**: PNG when transparency needed

**Dimensions**:
- **Hero Images**: 1920x1080 minimum, 3840x2160 preferred
- **Section Graphics**: 1200x800 to 1600x900
- **Icons/Small**: 512x512 or 800x600

**Optimization**:
- Use ImageOptim, Squoosh, or similar tools
- Target < 200KB for section graphics
- Target < 500KB for hero images
- Provide 2x (@2x) retina versions where beneficial

**Responsive Considerations**:
- Ensure important details aren't at edges (will crop on mobile)
- Key focal points should be within safe center area
- Test at multiple breakpoints (mobile, tablet, desktop)

---

## AI Tool Recommendations

**Midjourney**: Best for hero images and photorealistic scenes  
**DALL-E 3**: Excellent for technical illustrations and precise compositions  
**Stable Diffusion (SDXL)**: Good balance, local control, free  
**Adobe Firefly**: Commercial-safe, good for corporate imagery

**General Tips**:
- Generate multiple variations (4-8 per prompt)
- Use negative prompts to avoid: "text, watermarks, logos, people's faces, cluttered, busy, low quality"
- Upscale selected images before final use
- Color correct in Photoshop/Figma to match exact brand colors if needed
