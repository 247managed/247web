**Duration**: 30 seconds  
**Tone**: Confident, professional, warm but authoritative  
**Voice**: Male or female, mid-range, clear enunciation  
**Pacing**: Measured, not rushed. Natural pauses for emphasis.

### Script:

```
[0-6s]

247 Managed Network Solutions delivers.

[7-14s]
Senior engineering teams building production-ready software
with .NET 10, Blazor, SwiftUI, and React.

[15-22s]
From enterprise modernization to mobile apps,
we plug into your rhythm and ship maintainable code.

[23-30s]
Based in Fairfield, Iowa.
Available wherever excellence is required.
```

**Pronunciation Guide**:
- "247" = "Two forty-seven" (not "two four seven")
- ".NET" = "dot net"
- "Blazor" = "BLAY-zer"
- "SwiftUI" = "Swift U-I"

**Direction Notes**:
- Emphasize "delivers" (5s)
- Slight pause after "Senior engineering teams" (7s)
- Warm, confident tone on "Based in Fairfield, Iowa" (23s)
- End with subtle pride on "wherever excellence is required"

## Recording Specifications

**Technical Requirements**:
- **Format**: WAV or AIFF (lossless), 48kHz, 24-bit
- **Environment**: Studio-quality, no background noise
- **Processing**: Light compression and EQ, minimal reverb
- **Export**: MP3 320kbps for final delivery

**Voice Characteristics**:
- **Primary**: Professional, mid-range voice (male or female)
- **Age Sound**: 35-50 years old (experienced but not dated)
- **Accent**: Neutral American English (Midwest or West Coast preferred)
- **Delivery**: Clear, authoritative but warm, no hard sell

**Alternate Takes**:
- Record 2-3 takes of each script
- Vary pacing slightly between takes
- One take with more energy, one take more subdued
- Final selection in post-production

---

## Music & Sound Design Notes

**Hero Loop**: Ambient soundscape optional, subtle tech sounds (keyboard clicks, interface beeps). Very quiet if used - 5-10% volume max.

**Brand Intro**: Modern corporate music, uplifting but not cheesy. Electronic with organic elements. Think: Moog synthesizers + acoustic piano. 80-100 BPM. Start subtle, build energy through middle, resolve confidently.

**Tech Highlights**: Minimal music, more sound design. UI interaction sounds, successful build sounds, code completion chimes. Crisp and modern.

**Process Flow**: Rhythmic but calm. Each phase has its own sonic identity:
- Clarify: Collaborative sounds (whiteboard markers, paper shuffling)
- Plan: Digital precision (clicks, diagram connections)
- Build: Active development (typing, tests passing, commits)
- Stabilize: Completion confidence (deployment success, monitoring pings)
